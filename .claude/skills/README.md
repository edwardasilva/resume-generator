# Agent skills

Two skills vendored from outside projects, each with its license and credit kept in its folder. Both are used with a grain of salt: the eeprep style guide, `.github/STYLE.md` in https://github.com/edwardasilva/eeprep is the authority for words and figures on this project, and a scan from either skill is a list of candidates to check in context, not a list of edits to make.

| Folder | Source | License | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `unslop-prose/` | [theclaymethod/unslop](https://github.com/theclaymethod/unslop) by Clayton Kim | MIT | A longer catalog of formulaic phrases and structures, before-and-after examples, a fact-preservation contract, and Python scanners |
| `unslop-interface/` | [heydamir/unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky | CC BY 4.0 | A catalog of AI-looking interface tells and a redline order for spacing, color, icons, copy, type, layout, and visuals |

Both copies are from 9 November 2026. To refresh one, clone the source and copy its skill files over the folder, then put the note at the top of `SKILL.md` back.
